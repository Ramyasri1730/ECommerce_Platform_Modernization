# Project Charter

## Project Name
E-Commerce Platform Modernization & Customer Experience Transformation

## Vision
Create a reliable, transparent and personalized digital commerce experience from discovery through post-delivery support.

## Problem
The current platform has high cart abandonment, delayed processing, payment failures, inventory accuracy issues, fragmented tracking and manual returns.

## Objectives
- Cart abandonment <40%
- Order processing <2 hours
- Payment success >98%
- Inventory accuracy >99%
- Delivery SLA >95%
- Return processing <48 hours
- CSAT >4.7/5
- Repeat purchase >40%
- Conversion >4.5%

## Scope
Customer, catalog, cart, checkout, payment, order, inventory visibility, shipment tracking, returns/refunds, notifications, support and analytics.

## Out of Scope
ERP, warehouse automation, manufacturing, procurement and financial accounting.

## Delivery Approach
Agile Scrum with SDLC governance, incremental releases, continuous requirement validation and UAT.

## Key Stakeholders
CEO, Product Owner, Marketing, Sales, Customer Support, Warehouse, Logistics, Finance, Technology, QA, customers, delivery partners, payment gateway and vendors.

## High-Level Milestones
1. Discovery & initiation
2. Requirements
3. UX/process design
4. MVP development
5. Integration testing
6. UAT
7. Production release
8. Hypercare
