# Scope Statement

## In Scope
- Customer registration, authentication and profiles
- Address management and wishlist
- Product catalog, search, filters, reviews
- Recommendations
- Inventory availability
- Cart and coupons
- Checkout and shipping selection
- UPI, cards, net banking, wallets and COD
- Order placement and tracking
- Shipment updates and delivery confirmation
- Invoice generation
- Returns, refunds and replacements
- Notifications
- Customer support
- Sales, customer, marketing and inventory dashboards

## Out of Scope
- ERP implementation
- Warehouse automation
- Manufacturing
- Vendor procurement
- Financial accounting

## Scope Control
Any new capability must be assessed for business value, dependency, effort, risk and impact on release objectives before entering the backlog.
