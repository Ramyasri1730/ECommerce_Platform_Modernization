# Gap Analysis

| Area | Current State | Future State | Gap | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Checkout | Multi-step friction | Simplified guided checkout | UX/process | High |
| Payment | Multiple failures | Multi-method + retry | Integration/reliability | High |
| Inventory | Delayed updates | Near-real-time availability | Synchronization | Critical |
| Orders | 24–48 hr processing | <2 hr target | Automation | Critical |
| Tracking | Partial | End-to-end visibility | Logistics integration | High |
| Returns | Manual | Digital workflow | Automation | High |
| Marketing | Limited personalization | Contextual recommendations | Data capability | Medium |
| Reporting | Delayed/manual | Real-time operational dashboards | Analytics | High |
| Support | High status queries | Self-service tracking | CX capability | High |

## Priority Logic
Critical = direct revenue/order/inventory risk.
High = direct customer experience or operational efficiency impact.
Medium = optimization after core journey stabilization.
