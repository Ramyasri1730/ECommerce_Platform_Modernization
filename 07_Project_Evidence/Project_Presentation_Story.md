# Project Presentation Story

## Opening
XYZ Retail was growing rapidly, but customer and operational processes were not scaling at the same pace.
The main symptoms were 68% cart abandonment, 24–48 hour order processing, payment failures, inventory delays
and limited tracking.

## BA Approach
I started with business objectives and stakeholder analysis, mapped the current state, performed gap analysis,
designed the future state and translated needs into requirements and Agile user stories.

## Technical BA Analysis
I defined payment, inventory, logistics, notification and recommendation integration requirements and created
an ERD, DFD, data dictionary and CRUD matrix.

## Testing
I created an RTM linking requirements to user stories and test cases, with special attention to the payment
retry scenario where duplicate orders must be prevented.

## Business Impact
The transformation targets cart abandonment below 40%, processing below two hours, payment success above 98%,
inventory accuracy above 99%, delivery SLA above 95%, CSAT above 4.7 and repeat purchase above 40%.

## Key BA Learning
Modernization is not only feature delivery. A BA connects business problems, customer journeys, processes,
requirements, integrations, testing and measurable KPIs.
