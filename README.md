# E-Commerce Platform Modernization & Customer Experience Transformation

**Portfolio Case Study | Business Analyst | Agile Scrum / SDLC**

> **Project type:** Simulated BA portfolio project based on a realistic Indian e-commerce transformation scenario.  
> **Role:** Business Analyst  
> **Methodology:** Agile Scrum + SDLC  
> **Focus:** Customer experience, checkout conversion, order processing, inventory visibility, payments, returns/refunds, analytics and operational efficiency.

## 1. Executive Summary

XYZ Retail is a rapidly growing multi-category e-commerce business receiving **100,000+ daily website visitors** and processing thousands of orders every day. Despite sales growth, the existing platform creates friction across the customer journey and operational teams.

The transformation program aims to redesign the journey from product discovery through post-delivery support.

### Current-state pain points

| KPI | Current | Target |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cart abandonment | 68% | <40% |
| Order processing time | 24–48 hrs | <2 hrs |
| Payment success rate | 90% | >98% |
| Inventory accuracy | 88% | >99% |
| Delivery SLA compliance | 82% | >95% |
| Return processing | 7 days | <48 hrs |
| CSAT | 3.8/5 | >4.7/5 |
| Repeat purchase rate | 22% | >40% |
| Revenue conversion rate | 2.8% | >4.5% |
| Order tracking | Partial | 100% real-time |
| Support tickets | High | -40% |

## 2. Business Objectives

1. Reduce cart abandonment below 40%.
2. Reduce order processing to less than 2 hours.
3. Provide real-time inventory visibility.
4. Provide end-to-end shipment tracking.
5. Automate returns, refunds and replacement workflows.
6. Improve payment success above 98%.
7. Increase retention and repeat purchases.
8. Introduce personalized recommendations.
9. Provide executive operational dashboards.

## 3. Scope

### In scope
Customer management, product catalog/search, recommendations, reviews, cart, coupons, checkout, payments, order management, inventory visibility, shipment tracking, returns/refunds/replacements, notifications, customer support and analytics.

### Out of scope
ERP implementation, warehouse automation, manufacturing, vendor procurement and financial accounting.
