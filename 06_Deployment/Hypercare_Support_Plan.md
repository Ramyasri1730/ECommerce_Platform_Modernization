# Hypercare Support Plan

## Daily Monitoring
Orders, payment success/failure, inventory synchronization, shipment events, return/refund exceptions,
support ticket volume and dashboard freshness.

## Severity
P1 = revenue/order processing unavailable
P2 = major customer journey degradation
P3 = limited functional issue with workaround
P4 = cosmetic/minor issue

## Exit Criteria
No unresolved critical production defects, stable payment/order processing, stable inventory and tracking,
support team operating independently and business owner approval to move to BAU.
