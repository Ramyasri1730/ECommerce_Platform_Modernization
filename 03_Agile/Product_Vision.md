# Product Vision

**For** XYZ Retail customers and operations teams,

**the modernized commerce platform** will provide a fast, transparent and personalized shopping journey,

**unlike** the current fragmented platform,

**by** integrating checkout, payments, inventory, order management, logistics, returns and analytics around measurable customer and operational outcomes.

## MVP Priority
1. Checkout and payment reliability
2. Inventory and order automation
3. Real-time tracking
4. Returns/refunds
5. Operational dashboards

## Scrum Cadence
- Sprint duration: 2 weeks
- Daily Scrum: 15 minutes
- Sprint Planning: beginning of sprint
- Sprint Review: end of sprint
- Retrospective: end of sprint
- Backlog refinement: ongoing
